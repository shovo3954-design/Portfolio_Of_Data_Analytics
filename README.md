## 🔍 Project 1: Unlocking Sales & Profit Insights for Sustainable Growth

**Client:** XYZ Co.
**Period Analyzed:** 2014 – 2018

### 📌 Problem Statement
XYZ Co. needed a clear picture of what was actually driving their revenue and profitability. This project analyzes five years of sales data to answer that question — and to flag where performance was falling short of expectations.

### 🎯 Objectives
- Identify the key drivers of **revenue and profit** across products, sales channels, and regions
- Uncover **seasonal trends** in sales performance
- Spot **performance gaps and outliers** worth investigating
- Compare **actual results vs. budget targets** to measure how well the business hit its goals

### 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
- Python, Excel, SQL, Power BI

## 📊 Key Insights
- **$1.2B revenue** at a **37.36% margin** across 64K orders, with clear seasonality (dips in Feb/Jul, peaks in May/Nov).
- **Wholesale drives 54% of revenue**, but **Export is the most margin-efficient channel (38%)** despite only 14.6% revenue share.
- **California leads all states (19.5% of revenue)**, and the **West region has the highest margin (37.5%)** — though profitability stays consistent (~37%) across all regions.
- No strong link between unit price/revenue and margin — profitability is broad-based, not concentrated in a single product, channel, or region.
